Cleanup (fixes fill leaks)
Hatch / stipple textures (stucco, stone, shake) are made of thousands of tiny disconnected dots. That breaks the fill tool's flood-fill, because it can only spread through pixels of one continuous, matching color. Cleanup removes the small dots and keeps the real boundary lines, so each area becomes one solid, fillable region.
